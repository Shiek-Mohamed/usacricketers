   255→### Tradeoff documented (token vs DB re-read):
   256→- The token issued at login embeds `tournamentIds` and is the sole auth credential the score-service will check during socket connection (orchestrator's contract). This is acceptable for the use case (admin usually edits assignments before a match starts) and matches the orchestrator's design.
